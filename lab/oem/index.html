We make bubble tea a science

Here in Yen Chuan, flavoured powders and syrups are more than just ingredients - they are a science, where every component, every molecule influences the overall sensory experience. From flavour and aroma to texture, colour and mouthfeel, every part counts to paint the perfect cup of bubble tea. ‍ With our professional R&D team, we carefully craft our powder/syrup formulae, aiming for the perfect representation of flavour, aroma and texture in every sip. For clients needing custom-made flavours, we test relentlessly to identify, extract and replicate the essence of the products as faithfully as possible.

Flavour matters

Flavour goes beyond the five basic tastes - it’s a complex interplay between taste and aroma to create each unique flavour profile. Broadly-speaking, flavour profiles can be grouped into a number of categories:

Fruity

Floral

Sugar/Desserts

Nuts and spices

How it works

Analyze

We analyze the target flavour as a composite of taste, aroma, mouthfeel/texture and colour

Identify

We match and identify the analyzation results with our database of established flavour components

Replicate

Using a variety of natural and artificial flavours, colours and other ingredients, we aim to replicate the taste experience as faithfully as possible

Testing

The resulting formula is tested rigorously, then further refined in order to match the intended results closely

Our professions

Research Lab

Our in-house lab features industry-grade equipment and anti-microbe controls, with accelerated shelf life testing capability. ‍ We maintain strict standards for our products, using hurdle technology to monitor food quality and safety. Our powders maintain a water activity of under 0.4, with moisture content under 9%; Fruit jams have a water activity of under 0.6, with pH under 4.6. ‍ We can also provide test reports when needed, rest assured that your goods are safe, high-quality and in best conditions!
