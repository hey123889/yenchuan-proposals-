HOW TO ORDER

Ordering from us is simple – just follow these steps:

Enquiry

Have any questions or want to order samples? Contact us!

Sample request

We offer small samples for free, but you will need to pay for delivery. Tell us what you want to try, and where to send them to!

Confirmation

Check with customs to make sure that the products can be imported, and you are allowed to import our goods.
If you asked for customized products, we’ll also provide samples for checking.

Quotation

Let us know which products you want to order, how much to order, and how you would like us to ship them to you. We will then provide a quotation.

Order

Once the order is confirmed, we’ll send you a PI (Proforma Invoice) for your order

Payment

Please pay according to the PI within 3 business days, we’ll confirm the payment within 5 business days. To help speed things up, kindly send us the receipt of your payment via email

Lead times

Depending on product type and quantities, it may take 2-3 weeks to produce the goods. We will inform you once the delivery date is confirmed.

After-sales service

We hope to maintain close contact with our customers. Have any feedback about our products or service? Let us know!
