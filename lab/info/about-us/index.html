About us (online store)

Yen Chuan is a professional Taiwanese bubble tea (boba tea) & beverage ingredient manufacturer, supplier, and trading company. Our company owns a professional fruit juice factory, a powder factory, a tea company, and a professional team. With our facilities and crew, we strive to provide a one-stop service for our customers.

Dedicated to the famous Taiwanese Bubble Tea, we have served the chain beverage industry for more than 20 years, supplying powders, syrups, tapioca pearls, tea leaves and so much more. We can also offer OEM/ODM services for our customers too!

In answer to market trends and customer demands, we’ve also hired more professional staff to develop new products and offer customization services. Not only for bubble tea, but also for other products: ready-to-drink beverages, jellies, desserts and more.

Armed with 2 decades of experience, we take high-quality Taiwanese ingredients and sell them in 20+ countries around the world, from North America and Europe back to our Asian and Southeast Asian brothers.
Upholding our motto, “Real products, real services, and true integrity”, we serve only the best Taiwanese bubble tea materials and other products for our valued customers.
