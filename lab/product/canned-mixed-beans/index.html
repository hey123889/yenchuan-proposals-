Canned mixed beans

Can’t decide on what beans to use? Why not all of them?

Our canned mixed beans contains a mix of 8 different kinds of beans, also known as “Babao” in Chinese.

Aside from adding to drinks and desserts, you can also make “Babao congee”, a healthy hot dessert using this product!

Request a quoteBuy a sample

Wholesale prices depend on volume and destination. Our sales team or AI assistant replies with MOQ, lead time and shipping options.

Details

Unit size: 3.3kg per can

Carton size: 6 cans per carton

Shelf life: 2 years

How to use: Can be mixed with ice cubes or milk for consuming, or added to drinks or desserts as toppings

To make babao congee: Cook glutinous rice and add the canned mixed beans. Add red dates and longyan and mix.
