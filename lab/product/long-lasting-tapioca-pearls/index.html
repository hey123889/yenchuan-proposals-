How to cook:

  1. Heat water in pot until boiling
  2. Add dried pearls to pot. Cook for 30 minutes while stirring.
  3. Turn off heat, place lid on pot
  4. Let steep for another 30 minutes
  5. Strain the pearls and soak in brown sugar syrup to add flavour
  6. Enjoy!
