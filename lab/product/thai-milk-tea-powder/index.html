Thai milk tea powder

SKU MT13

Thai milk tea is made by strong black tea, sometimes spiced, with concentrated milk and sugar added to it. The result? A strong, sweet exotic brew.

With our Thai milk tea powder, you don’t need to fly to Thailand to try it!

How to make a delicious cup of drink (500ml):

  1. Add 50g powder into cup
  2. Add 200ml water and mix
  3. Add syrup and toppings to taste then mix
  4. Top up with ice cubes
  5. Enjoy!
