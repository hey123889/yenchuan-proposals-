Tasty Terrific Taro jelly!

2023-07-07 · New products, News, Recipes

Bubble tea has been around for years, and many bubble tea shops have been creating new innovative drinks to stand above the crowd. One such example is taro jelly – transforming the classic taro flavour into jelly form!

By adding taro jelly to fresh milk, you’ll create a perfect mix of milk’s creaminess and taro jelly’s chewy, soft texture. In many ways it’s similar to grass jelly, but with taro’s rich flavour and sweetness!

Read on to learn more!

The magic of taro jelly

Taro is one of the most common and popular flavours in bubble tea, well-loved for its distinctive purple colour and mild, nutty flavour.

When in jelly form, taro gains a smooth, bouncy jelly texture. It lends a dash of colourful, playfulness to any drink, just drop a spoonful of jelly to your favourite drink to liven it up!

For example, once added to milk, the delicate, smooth texture of the jelly perfectly complements the milk’s rich flavours. Take a sip, savour the smooth jelly against your tongue, and enjoy the rest of the milk!

Give it a boost!

The best thing about taro is how it easily matches and elevates different flavours and textures. Want to add some extra oomph to your taro jelly latte? Give those options a try!

Boba pearls

Tapioca pearls and taro are a match made in heaven! The smoky, caramel-like flavours of brown sugar add a layer of complexity to the drink.

Every mouthful is a symphony of flavours and textures!

Coconut flakes

Want to add a tropical twist to your drink? Try adding toasted coconut flakes! The flakes add a delightful crunch to the drink, while its flavours perfectly complements the jelly.

One sip would transport you to a tropical paradise!

Caramel

For those with a sweet tooth, why not drizzle some caramel syrup onto the drink? Not only is it a visual spectacle, the rich buttery caramel is also a great match for both the milk and taro!
Enjoy the beautiful syrup patterns, then dive into the layered flavours!

This drink is an irresistible beverage that enchants with its blend of creamy milk and wobbly taro jelly, creating a truly delightful experience.

Whether you’re a fan of taro or just love exploring new and exciting beverages, this latte will surely captivate your heart!

Taro Jelly Latte

Venture into the realm of the Taro Jelly Latte and let it enchant your taste buds with its delightful magic.

What you’ll need:

– 10g taro jelly powder (6 servings)
– 400ml fresh milk
– 60ml hot water
– Ice cubes (optional)

How to make:

Step 1

Add powder and water to the bowl, and mix well.

Step 2

Refrigerate for about 1 to 2 hours or until completely set and chilled.

Step 3

Fill the glass with milk, ice cubes, and the jelly, then serve

Give it a try!

If you’re craving something special to quench your thirst, grab a bottle of milk, mix up some jelly and make youself this delightful beverage!
