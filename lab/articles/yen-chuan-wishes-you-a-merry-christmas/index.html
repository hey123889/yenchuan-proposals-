Yen Chuan wishes you a merry christmas!

2025-12-23 · News

Original public content from Yen Chuan. Product claims and specifications remain subject to confirmation by the company.

Merry Christmas!

As we wave goodbye to 2025 and usher in the New Year, what’s better to celebrate than a rich, creamy cup of bubble tea? Gather with family and friends, slurp up some boba, and let it sweeten your soul!

For example, why not whip up a cup of red velvet cake bubble tea?
The mix of jolly red colours and creamy white milk is perfect to get the Christmas mood going, while the rich chocolate flavour adds a spot of luxury.

Another classic combination is peppermint hot chocolate: Enjoy a creamy, decadent chocolate, and let the peppermint give you a refreshing pick-me-up!

What’s more, you can pair this drink with a layer of rich, foamy cream top, or add tapioca pearls for an extra chewy mouthfeel.

Thank you for the support so far, and we hope you’ll have a fantastic new year ahead!
