How to make Apple Jasmine Iced Tea

2022-08-22 · Recipes

Original public content from Yen Chuan. Product claims and specifications remain subject to confirmation by the company.

Jasmine tea is one of the most famous tea blends in the world, and with good reason: Its signature floral aroma makes it a top choice for drinking all year around!

What makes the apple jasmine ice tea so captivating? The juicy sweetness of apple and jasmine’s signature aroma are perfectly captured in the green tea, yet doesn’t overpower the tea’s own delicate layers of flavours.
These rich, evocative flavours blend and mingle to create a distinctive, fragrant combination!

Wanna throw the perfect tea party?
Try out this refreshing floral cold beverage, which will certainly brighten your day and make your summers blissful!

Jasmine Iced Tea with Apple

The perfect thirst quencher with a floral aroma and delicate flavor!

What you’ll need

– 1 apple cored, cut into thin wedges
– 12g green tea leaves
– 500g hot water
– 300g ice cubes
– 100g jasmine syrup

How it’s done:

Step 1

Put the apple wedges and 200g of hot water in a pot, cook until the pulp is soft and translucent, remove the pulp and turn off the heat.

Step 2

Put green tea leaves into the teapot and add hot water to steep for 2-3 minutes. Strain tea leaves and pour tea into a cup with ice.

Step 3

Stir in the apple juice and jasmine syrup. Serve.

Now you can have restaurant-quality iced tea at home.

Drink up and enjoy!
