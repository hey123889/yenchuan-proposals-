Choosing tapioca pearls for your shop: dried, frozen or instant?

2026-11-13 · AI-drafted demo

AI-drafted demo: the topic follows what leading suppliers write about; the content uses only Yen Chuan's own product data. On the live site every AI article is approved by Yen Chuan before publishing.

Tapioca pearls are the one topping almost every bubble tea shop needs, but "tapioca pearls" covers several quite different products. The right choice depends on how much time your team has, how much storage space you have, and how many cups you sell each day. Here is how the seven pearls in our range compare.

1. Dried pearls: the classic choice

Dried black tapioca pearls are the most common option. They come in 3kg bags, 6 bags per carton, and are cooked in your shop. We also make brown and white pearls in the same 3kg format, so you can offer more than one colour on your menu.

Good for: shops with a steady flow of customers and staff who can cook pearls in batches.

2. Frozen dried pearls: longer storage

Frozen dried tapioca pearls come in the same 3kg bags (6 per carton) and are frozen for a shelf life of 24 months, with no preservatives added. They suit shops that order in larger quantities and have freezer space.

3. Instant pearls: ready in minutes

If your team does not have time to cook, instant pearls are the shortcut:

  • Instant tapioca pearls (with syrup) — in 100g packs (100 per carton) or 1kg packs (20 per carton), with a 12-month shelf life.
  • Instant tapioca pearls (room temperature) — 75g packs, 120 per carton, MOQ 90 cartons, stored at room temperature.

Good for: kiosks, cafés adding bubble tea as a side menu, events, and new shops still training staff.

4. Long-lasting pearls

Our long-lasting tapioca pearls are made for a longer shelf life, with an MOQ of 50 cartons — an option for distributors and chains that need stock to travel further.

How to decide

Your situationStart with
Busy shop, trained staffDried pearls (black, brown or white)
Large orders, freezer spaceFrozen dried pearls
Little time or no kitchenInstant pearls
Long shipping or distributionLong-lasting or room-temperature instant pearls

Not sure? Ask for samples of two or three types and test them with your own recipes and equipment. Our team can also suggest syrups that pair with each pearl.
