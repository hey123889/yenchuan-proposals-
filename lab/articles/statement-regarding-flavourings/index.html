Statement regarding flavourings

2020-08-07 · News

Dear our most valued customers:

Food safety is one of our most important concerns, and we strive to provide products of the highest quality,
with accordance to Taiwanese food regulations.

We hereby declare that our products do not contain the following synthesized flavourings:

  • Benzophenone
  • Eugenyl methyl ether
  • Pulegone
  • Styrene
  • Ethylacrylate
  • Myrcene
  • Pyridine

Your safety is our promise

Signed,
