A message to our customers about the coronavirus

2020-03-25 · News

Dear our most valued customers,

We understand that this is a challenging time for everyone, and our hearts go out to you, your family and others in need.
We would also like to extend a heartfelt thank you to health workers worldwide, who have worked hard to combat this outbreak.

We are continuing our business operations as usual, while following government health guidelines and policies.
Our production schedules are not affected at the moment, but we do apologize in case of any delays.

We understand that some of you may worry about shipping schedules, therefore we would like to offer free storage services:
We can store your ordered goods in our warehouses free of charge, until the outbreak ends.

Thank you for your continuous support, and we’ll be here for you in these trying times.
If you have any questions or request, feel free to contact us.

Please take care, and stay safe.

Sean Chen, CEO
