Alcohol-free alcoholic flavours – enjoy the booze, no more hangovers!

2022-12-15 · New products, News

Whenever there’s a party going on, it’s almost certain that the alcohol will flow – from wines and spirits to cocktails and champange, boozy drinks are a staple in celebrations almost worldwide!

However when the merrymaking’s over and you wake up the next day with a throbbing headache, it dawns on you that you’re having the dreaded hangover.
Nobody likes hangovers, especially when you have a whole day of work ahead of you. What can you do?

And that’s only for the people who do drink alcohol. What if you’re a teetotaler (or is allergic to alcohol), but want to join the the fun?

Time for our alcohol-free alcoholic flavours to shine!

What’s so special about them?

Exactly what it says on the tin, our alcohol-free alcoholic flavours boast the same taste and smell of the real deal, but without a single drop of alcohol.

Now you can enjoy the same party drinks you crave, without worrying about getting drunk. While some might say that it’s all part of the experience, at least you can wake up feeling refreshed rather than nursing a headache!

What flavours are there?

We currently offer the following products:

Beer flavoured cream top powder
Umeshu flavoured syrup
Kir flavoured powder

Beer flavoured cream top powder

The classic fluffy cream top you find in bubble tea, but with a boozy-flavoured kick!

Add a spoonful of this foam into your morning coffee, and transform it into a foamy bee latte!

Umeshu flavoured syrup

Sweet, tart with a hint of faux-alcohol, this syrup is the straight-laced’s way to enjoy this Japanese favourite!

Try pairing it with soda water, or with juices such as lemon or pineapple!

Kir flavoured powder

The classic French cocktail, now in powdered form! With a perfect mix of blackcurrant and alcohol flavours, one sip can take you to the French vineyards!

Want a bubbly kick to it? Stir this powder into soda water!
