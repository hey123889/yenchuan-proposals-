Enjoy a sparkly, sweet treat with peach soda!

2023-01-26 · News, Recipes

Everybody loves peaches, spring, and warm weather!

As springtime is just around the corner and the spring flowers are itching to bloom, it’s time to mix up some sparkling peach soda to satisfy your bubbly cravings.

Simple and refreshing, this drink gives your usual sparkling water a fabulous and flavorful twist!

Try bubbly peach soda!

Sparkling peach soda is easy to make, while delicious and healthy to boot – the perfect alternative to soda for weight-watchers who crave a refreshing treat.

All you need are some sparkling water, juicy fresh peaches, and syrup. The result? A delightful drink with a sweet, aromatic zing! 

The drink showcases the sweet taste of peaches, with a lively, bubbly mouthfeel that tingles your tastebuds. Imagine yourself on a relaxing spring break as you sip this fruity soda from your porch, backyard, or dinner table.

This versatile drink can be enjoyed at any time of the day, whether for a refreshing morning pick-me-up, barbecue, or brunch on the patio. The combination of sweet ripe peach flavor and refreshing bubbles is one that cannot be beat.

Why not give it a try and see for yourself? Maybe you’ll discover a new favourite treat to sip on!

Let’s sit back, relax, and sip away!

Sparkling Peach Soda

Savor the luscious kiss of peach in this fresh, fizzy fusion.

What you’ll need

– 500ml sparkling water
– 100ml peach syrup
– Ice cubes (optional)
– Canned/Fresh peaches (halved, pitted, diced) to taste

How to make:

Step 1

Fill glass with sparkling water

Step 2

Pour in peach syrup and stir well

Step 3

Add the peach slices and ice cubes to taste, then serve!

Tip: Garnish with a sprig of fresh mint if desired.

Want something more?

Feel free to experiment by using different fruit or sweeteners!

For example, add 1 1/2 tablespoons of grenadine and a splash of lemon or lime juice to make a Peach Sunrise. The possibilities are endless!
