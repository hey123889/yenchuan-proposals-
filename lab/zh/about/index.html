About

YenChuan

Yen Chuan is a professional Taiwanese bubble tea (boba tea) beverage ingredient manufacturer, supplier, and trading company. Our company owns a professional fruit juice factory, a powder factory, a tea company, and a professional team. With our facilities and crew, we strive to provide a one-stop service for our customers.

Dedicated to the famous Taiwanese Bubble Tea, we have served the chain beverage industry for more than 20 years, supplying powders, syrups, tapioca pearls, tea leaves and so much more. We can also offer OEM/ODM services for our customers too! In response to market trends and customer demands, we’ve also hired more professional staff to develop new products and offer customization services. Not only for bubble tea, but also for other products: ready-to-drink beverages, jellies, desserts and more.

Armed with 2 decades of experience, we take high-quality Taiwanese ingredients and sell them in 20+ countries around the world, from North America and Europe back to our Asian and Southeast Asian brothers. Upholding our motto, “Real products, real services, and true integrity”, we serve only the best Taiwanese bubble tea materials and other products for our valued customers.

Our Vision

We strive for innovation, to stand on the cutting edge of the industry by developing new products in response to the constantly-changing market. This is made possible with our in-house lab and our expertise with powders and syrups. Naturally, we also provide customization services for our clients who wish for a more unique profile. Want to stand out among your competitors and have flavours to truly call your own? YenChuan is here to help! Quality and creativity, that is the YenChuan way!

Our Value

QUALITY

We strive to provide high-quality bubble tea products, with carefully-sourced raw materials and rigorous quality control, all supervised by our team of professionals. Every step matters when it comes to ensuring our customers are fully satisfied.

NATURAL INGREDIENTS

We use natural flavourings and colourings where possible, and a certified nutritionist takes part in developing our drinks.

SERVICE

Customer service is key to happy clients. We love to hear our customers out and help them reach their goals. ‍ Need anything from us? Let us know and we are happy to help!

INNOVATION

We love to think out of the box, to innovate and to improve. We create new products constantly in response to market trends, or as a solution to age-old problems. ‍ We don’t stop at just following the leader, we want to be pioneers!

ECO-FRIENDLY

We help promote a more sustainable lifestyle with our series of Eco-friendly packaging and utensils. ‍ Join us in helping to save our planet, one step at a time!
