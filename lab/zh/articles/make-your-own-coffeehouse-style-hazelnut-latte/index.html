Make your own coffeehouse-style hazelnut latte!

Cold weather means hot drinks are in order! If you’ve ever been to Starbucks or any other coffeehouse, hazelnut lattes should be no stranger to you. This sweet, nutty drink is a great way to warm up on a chilly afternoon.

What if you don’t have time to visit your local cafe? Why not make it at home? It’s simple, affordable, and you can tweak the ingredients to your liking. Skip the queue in the cafe, stay in and make this delicious, classic treat yourself! 

Making this drink only takes 5 minutes of your time, and the results are just as, if not better than what you’d get in a cafe!

This delightfully lightly sweet & nutty latte goes well with Milano cookies or almond biscotti (dunk them into your drink before eating for the perfect experience), or you can splash in some espresso for a quick caffeine-boost!.

Staying in during winter? Slip into a pair of fuzzy slippers and a wooly sweater, brew up a warm cup of hazelnut latte, and wrap up on a couch with your favourite holiday films.

It’s guaranteed to make you feel warm and cozy on any day, time to add that toasty hazelnut kick to your daily routine!

Ready to begin? Check out the recipe!

Coffeehouse-Style Hazelnut Latte

Savor every drop of this deliciously nutty latte that will shake up your morning!

What you’ll need

– 400ml hot milk
– 100ml hot water
– 50g hazelnut syrup

How to make

Step 1

Fill a glass with hot water and hazelnut syrup and stir

Step 2

Pour in hot milk

Step 3

Enjoy!

Want to enjoy it with a dash of creamy sweetness?
Add a dollop of whipped cream on top – feel free to drizzle on some extra hazelnut syrup for more flavour!

And there you have it, a rich, creamy cup of delicious hazelnut latte!
