Enjoy the oatly goodness with Brown Sugar Oat Milk Boba!

Looking for a perfect treat to enjoy on a hot summer day? Or a healthy yet delicious drink to satisfy your sweet tooth cravings? Our brown sugar oat milk boba is guaranteed to brighten your day!

Tiger-striped goodness

You may have seen it at your favorite boba shop- Brown sugar milk boba, also known as Tiger Milk Tea Boba, is a Taiwanese drink that is popular all across the globe for its alluring flavors.

This popular beverage made it big for its sweet caramel taste, silky texture, and aesthetically pleasing colors. Brown sugar and milk mingle to create the pattern that gives its name, resulting to a wonderful harmony of flavours!

Instagrammable!

The same eye-catching aesthetic is what puts this drink on the map. With beautiful brown sugar stripes on a canvas of white milk, this drink is one of the most Instagrammable milk teas!

Don’t believe us? Pull up the app and check out the tags – there’s no mistaking this drink in its tiger-striped glory!

Why oat milk?

Being vegan or lactose-intolerant shouldn’t get in the way of enjoying a delicious cup of bubble tea.

Want to stave off the cravings with vegan-friendly options? Try plant-based milk! With its creamy texture and slight oatmeal-like flavour, oat milk is a great match for milk teas and boba milk alike.

What’s more, oat milk is also a healthy milk substitute! Every cup is chock-full of calcium, potassium and dietary fibres, while having lower levels of saturated fats compared to cow’s milk.

Now, you can whip up this iconic drink in your kitchen anytime! Deliciously sweet, fun to drink, simple to prepare, and vegan-friendly – made with oat milk or any non-dairy milk you prefer.

Brown Sugar Oat Milk Boba

Skip the queue in crowded boba shops by making your very own tiger milk boba at home!

What you’ll need

– 500g Oat milk
– 100g Tiger dot syrup
– 300ml Hot water
– 100g Ice cubes
– 1 sachet Ready-to-eat boba

How to make

Step 1

Drizzle the syrup on the glass, tip the glass to the side, and slowly roll until the sides are fully covered by syrup.

Step 2

Fill glasses with ice and pour in the oak milk. Make sure to avoid touching the sides of the cup.

Step 3

Steep the boba in the water for appx. 3 minutes. Pour the boba into the oat milk and serve.

Ready to begin?

Want to enjoy delicious bubble tea, but don’t want to wait in line?
Making your brown sugar oat milk boba at home is delightfully simple, and is a great activity to enjoy with your loved ones.

This recipe is perfect for a mid-afternoon break. Sweet, creamy, chewy, and lots of fun to make and drink!
