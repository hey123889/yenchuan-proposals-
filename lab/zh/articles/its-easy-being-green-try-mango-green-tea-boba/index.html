What’s better than a simple iced tea? Try mango green tea – a tasty, refreshing twist on the classic green tea!

Why mango green tea?

Combining the sweet, tropical tastes of mango with green tea’s more mellow and earthy tones, this perfect balance of flavours will leave you wanting for more!

Moreover, the drink’s eye-catching golden-yellow colour is a visual spectacle. Perfect for any hot summer afternoon with family and friends!

Want to boost the mango green tea even further? Top it up with some tapioca pearls! This is not any other tapioca pearls – instead we’ll be using mango-flavoured boba, a perfect match for this fruity drink!

With tropical floral undertones and a little bit of citrusy sweetness that’s addictive and downright delicious, this beverage is an irresistible fruity summer treat.

It invigorates your body and soul, and keeps you brimming with energy on a sweltering day!


Need something cooling and energy-giving? This drink is here to the rescue!
With just enough caffeine to get you over the midday slump. This tea will quench your thirst and cool you down when things get hot outside.

Furthermore, this drink is easily customizable! Tweak the amount of syrups or boba to your liking, or experiment with fruit jellies, coconut milk or other extras to make this drink your own!

Want to sip on something mangoliciously cool? Give the mango green tea a try – you won’t be disappointed!

Mango green tea boba

This delightful summer drink with bright, bold mango flavor will transport you straight to the tropics!

What you’ll need

– 12g loose leaf green tea
– 100g Mango syrup
– 600ml Hot water (about 70-85℃)
– 300g Ice cubes
– 1 sachet instant mango pearls

How to make

Step 1

Pour 300ml of hot water over green tea leaves in a pitcher; brew for 2-3 minutes. Strain tea leaves and adds ice for rapid cooling.

Step 2

Stir in the mango syrup

Step 3

Steep the boba in the remaining water for approx. 3 minutes. Pour the boba into the mango green tea and serve

Want something more? Add milk or a scoop of mango, or add ice-cream to transform this drink into a creamy treat!
